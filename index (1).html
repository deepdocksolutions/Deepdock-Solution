Services

Everything your fleet's data needs.

From the first PMS entry to the last spare on the shelf, done by people who know ships.

PMS database services

Set up right.

We build your planned maintenance database from the makers' manuals, with the right jobs, intervals and spares linked to the right equipment. We also audit existing databases, set up newbuilds and takeovers, and move data between systems.

Inventory management and audits

Know what's on board.

We count and match your stores, on board and in the warehouse, against what your system says. The result is fewer duplicate orders, clearer reorder levels and a stores list your crew can trust.

Digitisation

Every manual, searchable.

We turn manuals and drawings into searchable, indexed libraries, so a crew member finds the right page in seconds, not by asking whoever was last on board.

Back-office support

The routine work, handled.

A PMS desk for your fleet: keeping libraries and jobs current, answering ship and office queries, and producing the reports your office needs.
